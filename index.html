Published research

Zero Shot Low Light Image Enhancement using Vision Language Models and Semantic Diffusion

Kashinath Remeshkumar, Abhijith R, Kevin Varghese Theveril, Hema H., Dan Philip Bobby. 2026

A CLIP-guided semantic diffusion pipeline, built on Stable Diffusion v1.5, that brightens low-light images without paired training data or fine-tuning. A semantic-consistency filter rejects outputs that drift from the original scene.
